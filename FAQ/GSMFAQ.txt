#%TITLE:GSM FAQ

#%QUESTION:Can I send messages > 160 characters to a GSM phone?
#%ANSWER:In a single fragment, the maximum length is 160x7bit or 140x8bit characters to a GSM phone. But if you use UDHI and have a GSM phone that supports concatenated messages, you can transmit message fragments such that the GSM phone on receiving these fragments can present one larger message to the end user. <p>Some Message Centers may even support large messages and allow an ESME submit message payloads using the message_payload optional parameter in submit_sm or data_sm operations. The large text buffer is then automatically fragmented by the MC and delivered to the handset using UDHI.

#%QUESTION:How can I send ringtones, picture messages, UDHI, EMS?
#%ANSWER:GSM supports an enhanced message encoding mechanism called UDH or User Data Header. This mechanism is defined in the ETSI GSM documents (www.etsi.org). SMPP supports UDH in the form of a UDHI (I for Indicator) bit in the esm_class. <p>If an ESME submits a message with bit 0x40 (01000000) set, then it indicates to the MC, that the message contains UDH formatted data. The MC will carry this attribute right through to the mobile termination stage and the end result should be the delivery of the UDH-formatted message.<p>For Mobile originated delivery, an MO message carrying UDH data and delivered to an ESME via deliver_sm or data_sm, will present this indicator with bit 0x40 set in the esm_class.<p>UDH encoded messages can carry message fragments from a larger message, ring tones, picture messages etc. There are two subsets of UDH encodings used in the industry. <p>Nokia smart messaging is the most deployed and widely known and is featured on Nokia handsets only. Other vendors such as Motorola, Ericsson & Siemens are now either supporting or planning to support EMS (Enhanced Messaging Service) which is the official ETSI-approved standard for sending ring tones, picture messages & rich text.<p>UDH content is encoded in the short_message field of the submit_sm PDU or in the message_payload TLV. The content and its formatting is beyond the scope of this FAQ.<p>The Nokia Smart Messaging specification is available from Nokia (www.nokia.com) and the EMS specification is included in the ETSI GSM standards (www.etsi.org).
